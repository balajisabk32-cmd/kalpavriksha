# Kalpavriksha — product

**What it is:** a free learning portal run by Kalpavriksha Seva Trust for Tamil Nadu State Board (Samacheer Kalvi) students, Classes 4–12, in Tamil and English medium. Live at learn.kalpavrikshasevatrust.org.

**Who uses it**
- Students (main users): many on low-cost Android phones and slow mobile data; some on school smart panels. Sign in with a username and password their teacher gives them.
- Teachers and the trust office: create students, publish content, track progress.

**What students do:** open their class's subjects and chapters; read the explanation, watch the chapter's videos, open the textbook pages; practise every exercise (2.1, 2.2 …) and book-back question with step-by-step answers; take 10-question chapter quizzes; daily review of past mistakes. Also Holistic Growth (learner card/profile) and GK & Patriotic Songs.

**Language:** every screen in Tamil and English. Tamil text is longer; layouts must take it.

**Tone:** warm, encouraging, plain words. A trusted school, not a startup.

**Constraints:** fast first load on cheap phones (no WebGL, particle canvases or heavy animation on core screens); readable in sunlight; works on projectors; respects reduced motion; free, no ads.

**Visual direction (chosen 2026-11):** clean and light by default, like Khan Academy: paper-white surfaces, one calm purple brand colour, real product UI instead of decoration. Dark mode is a calm deep purple with the same layout. No glow, particles, 3D globes or scroll effects.
